How it works

Compatibility, not certainty.

Common Ground is a prototype matching tool. It does not diagnose anyone, make mental-health judgments, or guarantee relationship success. Personality information is one part of a broader compatibility model.

Signed-in profiles are saved to the Common Ground Supabase database. Demo mode still uses local browser storage. Messaging and verification are being connected in stages, with privacy and safety controls remaining a core requirement.
